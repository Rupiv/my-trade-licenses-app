How to obtain a new trade licence online

Before you apply
  1. Create an account with your mobile number. It is verified with an OTP.
  2. Sign in using the OTP sent to the same mobile number.
  3. Keep the {{ mandatoryDocumentCount }} mandatory documents ready as {{ documentRules.acceptedFormat }} files (see Required Documents).

After you submit
  1. Pay the licence fee online. The payment acknowledgement is available once the payment is confirmed.
  2. The application is reviewed, and the premises may be inspected, by the approving officers of your jurisdiction. You receive SMS updates along the way.
  3. Follow every action in Track Application.
  4. When the application is approved, download the licence certificate (PDF) from Track Application.
